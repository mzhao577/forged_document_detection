Procedure Note: Esophagogastroduodenoscopy
(EGD)

Procedure Details

Endoscope Model: ScopeCo Model 100. Route: Oropharyngeal entry, UES passage.
Patient Position: Left lateral decubitus. Bite Block: Applied.

Narrative

The scope traversed the oropharynx and comfortably passed through the UES. The
esophagus was visualized, notable for a normal Z-line at 40cm. GEJ at 45cm from
incisors. Mild erythema noted, no rings or strictures apparent. The stomach was
entered, revealing the body, fundus, and antrum in good view with insufflation. Rugal
folds were normal. Mild non-erosive gastritis observed in the gastric body. Pylorus open
and easily passable. Entered duodenum, bulb and second portion visualized, villi intact.
No ulcers or active bleeding seen. Retroflexion in the stomach to assess cardia/fundus
was unremarkable.

Findings
* Mild erythema at the GEJ
* Non-erosive gastritis in gastric body

Impression
Mild GERD changes. Non-erosive gastritis.

Diagnosis
* Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD)
* Non-Erosive Gastritis

Interventions
No biopsies or interventions performed during this procedure.
