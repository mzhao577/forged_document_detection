Procedure Note: Esophagogastroduodenoscopy
(EGD)

Procedure: The patient was positioned in the left lateral decubitus position. A bite
block was placed. Using a Olympus CF-H180AL endoscope, we carefully traversed
the oropharynx and passed the upper esophageal sphincter.

Esophagus: The esophageal mucosa appeared normal. The Z-line was identified at
38 cm from the incisors. The gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) appeared normal
without any evidence of rings, strictures, or esophagitis.

Stomach: Upon entering the stomach, the fundus, body, and antrum were examined.
Notable for mild erythema in the antrum, which was consistent with mild gastritis.
Normal rugal folds with adequate insufflation. The pylorus was easily traversed.

Duodenum: The first and second parts of the duodenum were examined. The villi
appeared normal. No ulcers or significant inflammation were observed.

Retroflexion: Retroflexed view of the cardia and fundus appeared normal.
Findings:

* Mild erythema in the gastric antrum, suggestive of mild gastritis.

Impression: The findings are consistent with mild gastritis. No evidence of Barrett's
esophagus or other significant pathology was noted.

Diagnosis: Mild gastritis.
Interventions: No biopsies were taken.
