Upon entry through the oropharynx, the endoscope was carefully advanced through the upper esophageal sphincter. The esophagus was inspected: mucosa was normal in color and texture. The Z-line was noted at 40 cm from the incisors, with a normal gastroesophageal junction (GEJ). There were no rings or strictures observed.

In the stomach, the fundus, body, and antrum appeared normal under insufflation. Rugal folds were distinct, with mild erythema noted in the antral region. No erosions or ulcers were identified. The pylorus was patent.

Examination of the duodenal bulb and second portion revealed normal mucosal pattern with visible villi. There was no evidence of inflammation or ulcers.
