Procedure Note: Esophagogastroduodenoscopy (EGD)

On 2023-01-18, Esophagogastroduodenoscopy was performed by Dr. David Proteus, MD, on patient positioned in left lateral decubitus position. An Olympus CV bronchoscope was used. A bite block was placed to protect the scope and patient's teeth.

Oropharynx visualized without abnormalities, swiftly passed into the esophagus through UES. In the esophagus, the Z-line was irregular, and observed approximately at 40 cm from the incisors. No stigmata of variceal disease or stricture noted. Presence of scattered mucosal erythema near the distal esophagus.

Entered the stomach after visualization of the GEJ junction. With ample air insufflation, rugae were observed in the body and antrum. Mild to moderate erythematous changes at the antrum consistent with gastritis. Pyloric channel was open upon approach in duodenum.

Duodenum explored to the second part revealing well-defined villi. Upon retroflexed vision, assessed gastric inlet for inflammation/bulges, results inconclusive but could denote inactive lesions.

Findings

  • Esophagitis characterized intermittently throughout distal esophagus.
  • Mild erythematous gastritis in distal gastric antrum.
  • Duodenum appears normal without evidence of erosion.

Impression

Chronic dyspepsia related to mild gastritis. Reassure and med compliance.

Diagnosis

  • Gastroesophageal reflux disease
  • Mild gastritis

Interventions

Biopsied sites include: lower esophagus, fundus and antral areas using cold forceps technique with specimens procured.
