Procedure Description

Upon entering the oropharynx, the endoscope was advanced with ease through the upper esophageal sphincter. The esophagus was carefully navigated, noting no varices or significant esophagitis. The Z-line was located 38 cm from the incisors, with the gastroesophageal junction (GEJ) appearing intact. In the stomach, the examination of the fundus, body, and antrum revealed mild erythema, most notably in the antrum. No ulcers or active bleeding observed. Retroflexion in the stomach provided clear visibility of the cardia and fundus without additional findings. Upon entering the duodenum, the bulb and second portion appeared normal with intact villi and no evidence of ulceration.

Findings

  • Duodenum: Normal, no lesions.
